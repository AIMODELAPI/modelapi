How payment works

  • Step 1 — Register: create an account with your email address and receive 50 free trial credits.
  • Step 2 — Choose a package: pick the monthly plan that fits your needs. Your account is topped up with that package's credits each billing cycle.
  • Step 3 — Generate: create images or videos. The corresponding credits are deducted only when a result is successfully delivered.
  • Step 4 — Track usage: view your balance, transaction history, and remaining credits in your dashboard at any time.
