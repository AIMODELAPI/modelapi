About ModelAPI

Our mission

ModelAPI makes AI infrastructure simple. We are a subscription-based, bring-your-own-key (BYOK) orchestration platform — one OpenAI-compatible endpoint, smart caching, and usage management, so our customers can build on AI without wiring up providers one by one.

What we do

  • Provide a unified API endpoint compatible with the OpenAI SDK.
  • Help customers optimize their own provider spend through Harness smart caching.
  • Offer subscription plans with clear, transparent pricing — we do not resell model tokens.

Our team

ModelAPI is operated by a team with 10+ years of overseas operations experience since 2012.
