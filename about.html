About ModelAPI

Our mission

ModelAPI makes AI creativity simple. We are a dedicated AI image and video generation platform — type a description and turn it into high-quality images and videos, powered by the Wan 2.7 model. Our goal is to let everyone create visuals without learning any technical skills.

What we do

  • AI image generation from text descriptions (1:1, 16:9, 9:16).
  • AI video generation from text descriptions (3 / 5 / 10 seconds, 480P / 720P).
  • A secure account system that tracks your generation history and usage.
