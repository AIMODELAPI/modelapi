FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to try?
Yes. Every new account receives 50 free trial credits on signup — enough for 5 images or a short video. No credit card required.
How does pricing work?
We use a credit system: 1 credit = $0.01. An image costs 10 credits; video costs 25 credits per second (720P) or 35 credits per second (1080P). Pick a monthly plan on the Pricing page that fits your volume.
What can I create?
Images in 1:1, 16:9 or 9:16 ratios, and videos of 3, 5 or 10 seconds in 720P or 1080P — all from a plain text description.
Can I use the results commercially?
Yes. Content you generate with your account may be used for personal and commercial purposes, subject to our Terms of Service.
What happens if a generation fails?
Credits are only deducted when a generation succeeds. Failed generations are never charged.
How long are my results available?
Download links are valid for about 24 hours. Please save your images and videos promptly after generation.
